The first public testing demo for Beetleworx on Windows.

Extract the ZIP and open `beetleworx.exe`. Setup accepts your Epic Mickey 2 Xbox 360 disc image or extracted files. For the Epic Mickey preview, also select your Wii RVZ image. The download contains no disc images or original game archives.

The demo includes the native PC options menu, mouse and controller rebinding, single-player character switching, movement abilities, the level browser, and Support & Updates. Mickey's original ink drips use assets from the mounted Wii disc.

Epic Mickey support is experimental; the courtyard is an exploration preview. Online play, HDR, and experimental scene lighting are disabled in this demo. Start at 30 FPS. See the [README](https://github.com/Bloticle/beetleworx#what-still-needs-work) for the rest of the known gaps.

Use **Support & Updates** to review and export a diagnostic report before opening an issue. Include the level and steps to reproduce the problem.

EMR movement animations: `@llenarox` on Discord.
