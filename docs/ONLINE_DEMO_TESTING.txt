EXPERIMENTAL ONLINE DEMO

The lobby supports a host plus fifteen guests. Large groups and full campaign
playthroughs remain unverified. The first two players currently use Mickey and
Oswald respectively; additional player character choices need broader testing.
Pants colors are unique in the lobby, but appearance and nametag rendering still
need multiplayer validation across areas and character combinations.

Online uses synchronized native inputs and world checks. Prediction, rollback,
world repair and reconnect are unfinished. A mismatch or lost peer can stop the
session. Arbitrary level changes, death/respawn and recovery are not proven
throughout the campaign. Keep a backup and report where a session stops.

Rooms currently require an invitation. Public matchmaking, joining games that
have already started, and voice through the internet relay remain unfinished.
Discord provides invitations and game launching; it is not the game's network
transport. Its in-game friends/invite picker is not implemented.

Text chat and gameplay use the room connection. The UI may still expose voice
controls, but do not rely on voice working through the relay in this build.
Use an ordinary Discord voice channel while testing with friends.

The original Epic Mickey mode is an offline courtyard exploration preview.
Full campaign progression, quests, scripts, transitions, audio, materials,
NPC animation, collision and spawning still need compatibility work.

Higher FPS can still show animation/camera/physics issues. Start at 30 FPS.
Experimental rendering upgrades and movement abilities need broader hardware
and area coverage. Imported Rebrushed movement clips are for Mickey; Oswald
uses existing animations. Mouse aiming and solo character switching/respawns
also need additional testing.

For a report, include your release version, area, what each player was doing,
number of players and reproduction steps. Support & Updates prepares a bounded
local report for review before you share it. Avoid posting room codes or private
save files publicly. No report is submitted automatically.
